Progress:
BF16 square root module finsihed. Now that mixuan nad vinay have mished the BF16 arithmetic things, a BF16 version of the sqaure root module has been finished. Though, not a parameterizable file due to slightly different logic, the FP16 and BF16 sqaure root modules now match what their models say. Both modules produce a ULP of 2 or less, which might be decreased in the future. A different algorithm can be used, however most iterative algorithms, the main one being newton-rhapson, require a division for each iteration. Quick modles show that to get to 1 ULP at least 2 iterations will have to be used. With the current iteration of the divider, that means at least 22 cycles of just division. This brings the instruction latency from around 13 cycles to the mid to upper 20s. I have determined that staying with the faster method will be benificial for now. If the workload study sees that we can pay for the longer instuction time, then in the future we could swicht algorithms.

Reduction unit will be a fast switch to BF16 once vaddsub is finalized. However, there is still the point of the area problems from the reduction tree. To achive the current functionality of broadcasts and partial write, the unit must hold the entire origional vectors for how many instructions are in flight. Synthesis shows that the FIFO required for this takes up around 40% of the modules total area. To reduce this, we have come up with the idea to still have it pipelined, but to limit the instructions in flight to 3-4. This means we still get performance benefits for paything the area costs for all the ALUs, however we reduce the size of the FIFO massively. With 4 FIFO stages, the area of the module was reduced by around 30%.
